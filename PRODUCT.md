# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (user choice). Static output, runs locally. A HyperFrames composition renders the hero loop video into `public/`.

## Users

People in Rome, locals and internationals, who want to start running or find a social running crew. They arrive from Instagram, a friend's invite or a race weekend, usually on a phone, and decide in seconds whether the club feels welcoming enough to show up.

## Product Purpose

Amor Run Club A.S.D. is a free, all-levels social run club in Rome. The site exists to get visitors to sign up: entry form (name, email, Instagram) plus acceptance of the Release of Liability. Secondary paths: WhatsApp community, Strava club, Instagram.

## Positioning

Running in Rome tied to Roman food culture: "Eat pasta run fasta." Runs end at pasta shops and social gatherings; the mascot is a running rigatone. Collaborations with local pasta makers and brands (Saucony "The Runner's Journal" edicola activation in Testaccio).

## Operating Context

Weekly group runs through Rome's streets; events such as EASY RUN 5K; updates published on Instagram (@amor_runclub). Meeting points include Piazza Testaccio area.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Entry form is a local demo: client-side validation only, no backend. Must require Release of Liability acceptance.
- Release of Liability text must be preserved verbatim (legal document, last updated April 7, 2025).
- Language: English.
- Undecided: exact weekly schedule, meeting point and times (do not invent; point to Instagram/WhatsApp).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Amor Run Club. Tagline: "Eat pasta run fasta." Headline voice: "Run, laugh and connect."
- Logo: retro rubber-hose cartoon rigatone with red headband and red sneakers, script "Amor Run Club" wordmark (`public/brand/logo.png`).
- Voice: warm, funny, inclusive, a bit cheeky. Never elitist about pace.

## Evidence on Hand

- Event and community photography in `public/photos/` (group shot in piazza, runs in the park, pasta shop, Saucony edicola activation).
- Real links: Instagram https://www.instagram.com/amor_runclub/, WhatsApp community, Strava club 1224988.

- No testimonials, member counts, pace stats or schedules on hand. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Every pace is welcome; the vibe sells, not performance.
2. Signing up must be one obvious, short action.
3. Real people and real Rome over stock imagery.
4. Legal text stays complete and readable, never hidden behind tricks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-operable form and dialog, reduced-motion support for video and animation.
